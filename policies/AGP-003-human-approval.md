# AGP-003 - Human Approval for High-Impact Actions

Before an agent takes an action that's destructive, hard to reverse, or
visible outside the organization (deleting records, sending messages,
deploying changes), a human must explicitly approve that specific action
first — unless a documented exception applies.

## Required controls

- Show the human exactly what the agent is about to do before they
  approve it.
- Keep a record of who approved the action, and when.
- Get a fresh approval if the proposed action changes — an old approval
  doesn't cover a different action.
